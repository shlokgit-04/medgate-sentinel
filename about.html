MedGate Sentinel

MedGate Sentinel reads the text of a lab report and does two jobs a busy reader often skips: it flags every value sitting outside the reference range printed on that same report, and it looks one step further, flagging combinations of related markers that together paint a pattern a single number would not.

Eight combination patterns are built in (uncontrolled blood sugar, iron-deficiency anemia, kidney function, thyroid, liver stress, lipid risk, vitamin D with calcium, and inflammatory response). A pattern only fires when several related markers are outside range in the matching direction, because one abnormal value alone rarely means much.

How it works

  • The analyzer runs entirely in your browser. Paste a report, and the parsing, comparison, and pattern matching all happen on your device.
  • Reference ranges are taken from your own report where it prints them, falling back to standard ranges only when the report omits them.
  • Create an account only if you want to save reports and revisit them later; without one, nothing leaves your browser.

Honest limits

This is a prototype built for a hackathon demonstration. It is not a medical device, it is not a diagnosis, and it does not know your history, your medications, or the hundred other things a doctor weighs. Its whole job is to make the report easier to read before you discuss it with one. Always consult a doctor.
